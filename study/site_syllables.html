We let a model from behavioural neuroscience (keypoint-MoSeq) split his movement into short, repeated pieces, "syllables", from the joints alone, knowing nothing about fighting.

  • Eight syllables cover nine-tenths of his time on his feet: the long stance, tall and light, stepping in, lunge and throw.
  • The same vocabulary in 2012 and 2021, in about the same proportions.
